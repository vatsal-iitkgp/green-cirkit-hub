On 31 March 2026, the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change notified the **Plastic Waste Management (Amendment) Rules, 2026**.

## The recycled-content ladder (Category I, rigid plastic packaging)

| Year | Minimum recycled content |
|---|---|
| FY 2025–26 | 30% |
| FY 2026–27 | 40% |
| FY 2027–28 | 50% |
| FY 2028–29 onwards | 60% |

Flexible packaging (Category II) starts lower, at 10% rising to 20%.

## Other changes worth knowing

- **Quality standard:** recycled plastic used in packaging must conform to **IS 14534:2023**.
- **Wider EPR net:** raw material suppliers are now covered by Extended Producer Responsibility.
- **Reuse targets** for rigid packaging appear for the first time.
- **Carry-forward relief:** a shortfall in FY 2025–26 recycled content for food-contact packaging can be carried forward for up to three years, since FSSAI rules limit recycled material there.

## Why it matters

Every brand selling rigid plastic packaging in India now needs a steady, certifiable supply of recycled resin, and that need doubles between FY26 and FY29. Collection, sorting and processing capacity has to grow fast to keep up. Buyers will favour suppliers who can prove origin and recycled content lot by lot.

According to BigMint, **25 producer price revisions** since the Gulf conflict began on 28 February 2026 have reset Indian polymer prices.

## The HDPE story

- **Blow moulding HDPE is up about 40%** over seven months.
- Prices **corrected in July**: three cuts took nearly ₹23/kg off blow moulding, to around ₹128.5/kg.
- A **renewed climb** began in late July as feedstock costs stayed well above pre-war levels.
- Middle East HDPE blow moulding imports were at about **$1,270/t CFR Mundra**, roughly 44% above pre-war levels.

## Why recycled matters more now

When virgin resin swings this hard, a stable domestic supply of near-virgin recycled HDPE becomes a hedge, not just a compliance tick. Recycled resin is made from local feedstock, so it is far less exposed to Gulf shipping and naphtha shocks.

India's polymer producers announced their 1 November 2026 revisions.

## What changed

- **Reliance (RIL):** HDPE injection and pipe grades **up ₹4/kg**. Other HDPE grades, including blow moulding, **unchanged**. LLDPE film also up ₹4/kg.
- **OPaL:** HDPE pipe and injection **up ₹2/kg**. Other HDPE grades unchanged.
- **IOCL recyclates:** R-HDPE white **up ₹1/kg**; R-HDPE black unchanged.

## Context

This follows RIL's 10 October revision, which raised HDPE pipe grades by ₹3,000/t and rolled over other HDPE grades. In mid-October, Delhi open-market HDPE blow moulding traded around **₹139/kg**.

Blow-moulding HDPE, the grade most packaging converters use, has held steady for now. Pipe and injection grades are firming. Track the weekly Delhi price on our [HDPE price tracker](/prices).
